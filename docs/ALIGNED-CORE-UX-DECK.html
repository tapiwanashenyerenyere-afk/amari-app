Product Direction

Aligned becomes the work room.

The tab is not a social feed. It is where members find projects, understand what people are building, save opportunities, make consent-based connections, and then contact each other through native phone apps.

Projects first Map and list Consent-based contact No in-app DMs yet
Core Rule

Show the work, not the private graph.

Members should see all approved projects and interests. They should only see deeper member context after a mutual connection, and should never see someone else's connection list.

Information Architecture

Four rooms inside Aligned.

Projects and Interests are discovery surfaces. Connections is the trusted relationship layer. My Board is the member's saved and submitted work.

Projects: approved work from the network, with map/list browsing.
Interests: what people are looking for, offering, or exploring.
Connections: mutual members only, with their work and contact actions.
My Board: saved projects, my projects, and my interests.

Contact Model

Use the phone's apps now. Log the intent only.

AMARI should not host private messaging until legal, moderation, retention, and governance rules are solved. For now, connected members can contact each other through email or phone messaging when a number is shared with an international code.

Log actor, target, channel, related project, and timestamp.
Require a short connection message before opening the native email app.
Do not store email body, message text, call records, or private notes.
Phone/message actions require explicit contact sharing. No vCard export.
Future: in-app messaging after governance decision.

Member Journey

The full Aligned path.

This is the journey we should optimise around: discover work, save it, connect with the person, then move the relationship into the real world through the phone's native apps.

Step 1

Browse

Member opens Aligned and browses projects by list or map.

Step 2

Understand

They open a project dossier and see the work clearly.

Step 3

Save

They save the project or request context without exposing contact data.

Step 4

Connect

They connect through QR, project, or interest consent.

Step 5

Contact

Email opens outside AMARI with the required connect note.

Success measure

A member should be able to answer, in under 30 seconds: what are people building, which projects matter to me, who can I speak to, and what is the next respectful action?

Build Plan

Ship the trust fixes before the magic.

Aligned can be ambitious, but the first implementation pass should remove privacy risks, clarify project browsing, then add scanner and admin controls behind hardened server functions.

1. Remove connection exposure from board and shared exports.
2. Rebuild Aligned navigation into Projects, Interests, Connections, My Board.
3. Add connection profile with projects, interests, and contact handoff.
4. Add QR scanner plus server-side connect RPC.
5. Add admin member directory, status actions, and invite issuing.
6. Build new iOS and Android releases because scanner is native.
Do Not Break
Invite security
No public code lists or direct client tier writes.
Auth paths
Keep OTP, magic link, Google, Apple, reviewer password.
Release rules
No unsigned production OTA. Android Play builds use GitHub signing.
Map compliance
Do not hide Mapbox logo or attribution.
