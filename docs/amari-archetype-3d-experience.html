Launch graph

Two-axis graph rendered as a premium 3D object.

This is likely the best v1 experience: understandable in one glance, tactile enough to feel AMARI-specific, and still useful for matching.

Member feeling

It feels like a choice, not a survey. The user drags the A and sees a living archetype label form in real time.

Data captured

Final X/Y, derived quadrant, time-to-settle, number of changes. No raw touch trail, no screen replay, no free text.

Build cost

Reanimated implementation is feasible quickly. Accessibility fallback can be quadrant chips and sliders.

Future graph

Six-axis octahedron for people who are blends, not boxes.

This is the more distinctive long-term direction. It maps Capital, Founders, Operators, Creators, Advisors, and Voices into a single spatial object.

How to explore

Drag the canvas to orbit. Use the six sliders on the left to move the AMARI point through the archetype space.

Member journey

The graph is only one moment in a five-step data experience.

The product should feel like self-discovery. The system behind it should remain disciplined: enum answers, consent, aggregates, and no unnecessary raw data.

1. What are you advancing in this season?

Venture Creative project Career move Investment thesis Community initiative

2. What has been hardest to find this year?

Capital Counsel Talent Peers Quiet

3. Where could you generously extend value?

Mentoring Introductions Hiring Investing Speaking

Use answers to personalize recommendations and understand the community in aggregate.

Let members edit later. Make the consent recap explicit before completion.

Never sell individual member data or expose member-level rows in sponsor reports.
